# 08 · Personal Learning Assistant Design → User Manual

## Learning Goals
1. Assemble the methods from the previous 7 lessons into your own AI learning-assistant way of working.
2. Design a three-in-one personal manual: "role setting + common templates + red lines."
3. Form a personalized learning system that is reusable long-term and iterable.
4. Make "academic integrity + fact-checking" a permanent clause of the manual.

---

## Warm-up Question
> In the previous 7 lessons you collected many good methods: guided questioning, mistake cards, revision records, checklists…
> But methods sit scattered and get forgotten once exams get busy. This lesson, we **bind them into a book** — your "AI Learning Assistant User Manual."
> From now on, for any learning task, just open the manual.

---

## Core Concepts

### 1. What is a "personal learning assistant"
Not a piece of software, but **a stable way of working you set up for AI**: it knows who you are, what tone to use, and what red lines to keep.

### 2. The three parts of the manual
- **Role setting (System Prompt)**: tell AI "you are my learning coach, guide me, don't give the final answer directly."
- **Common templates**: collect the templates from lessons 02/03/04/05/06/07, categorized by scenario.
- **Red-line clauses**: don't copy answers, check every fact first, note AI assistance, write the core content yourself.

### 3. Role-setting example
"You are my middle-school learning coach. For any question, first guide me to understand, don't give the final answer directly; for any factual info, remind me to check; I am ultimately responsible for what I submit."

### 4. The manual must be "alive"
Review it once a month; delete what doesn't work, add newly discovered good templates. It is your learning asset and grows more valuable over time.

## Real Learning-scenario Cases

### Case: One week before exams
Open the manual's "review plan" template, generate a weekly plan in 5 minutes; use the "self-test question" template for day-after self-tests; use the "checklist" to verify the exam-point data AI gave in the manual. The whole flow runs automatically.

### Case: Stuck writing an essay
Open the "writing revision record" template: write a draft yourself first, then go through the "mark 3 errors + explain" flow, and finally fill the three-column table. No more random asking.

## Right vs. Wrong Use

| | ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Method state | Scattered, relies on memory | Bound into a manual |
| Questioning | Made up each time | Apply fixed templates |
| Red lines | Often forgotten | Written in manual, reviewed often |
| Growth | Doesn't accumulate | Iterates and upgrades monthly |

## Hands-on Task
1. Write a "role setting" that states who you are and how you want AI to help (including guiding style + check reminder).
2. Organize the previous lessons' templates into the manual, categorized as "questioning / breakdown / writing / review / checking / speech."
3. Write 3–5 "red-line clauses" (must include: don't copy answers, check every fact first, note AI assistance).
4. Add a "monthly review" page to the manual, recording which templates work and which to change.

---

## Prompt Template (student version · role setting)
```
You are my [grade: ______] learning coach.
Please always:
1. Guide me to understand, don't give the final answer directly;
2. For any factual info, remind me to check before use;
3. Where I might directly copy, remind me "this is your homework, complete it yourself";
4. Use language I understand, with life examples when needed.
I am ultimately responsible for what I submit.
```
> Red line: the manual's first clause is always "don't copy answers, check every fact first, note AI assistance."

## Common Mistakes
1. **Manual too vague**: only slogans, no applicable templates.
2. **Missing red lines**: forgot to write integrity / checking in, easy to slide into ghostwriting.
3. **Write but never update**: manual becomes decoration, methods stay scattered.
4. **Role setting too broad**: let AI "help with everything," losing boundaries instead.

## Lesson Summary & Next Steps
Your "AI Learning Assistant User Manual" is the training camp's "final assembly": the role setting tells AI how to help you, the template set lets you apply instantly, the red-line clauses hold the bottom line, and the monthly review makes it better the more you use it. It is not a document but your personal "learning-method operating system."

### Advanced: digitize the manual
Beyond writing it in a doc, you can also:
- Save the role setting as the AI tool's "fixed prefix / system prompt," auto-attached to every conversation;
- Make common templates into phone-memo quick phrases, paste anytime;
- Use a table to record "which template for which task type," forming a retrieval index.
Let the manual truly blend into daily life, not be forgotten after writing.

### A note for parents/teachers
Have your child "demo" the manual to you once a quarter: open a conversation, paste the role setting, solve a real learning problem on the spot. You can directly see from their operation whether the method is internalized. Only when the manual comes alive does the camp truly end.

### Quick self-check card (10 seconds before each AI use)
- Did I attach my role setting? Attached → continue.
- Is this "guiding" not "ghostwriting"? Yes → continue.
- Did I hold the red-line clauses? Held → use with confidence.
The manual isn't finished by writing — it's "used into being." Use it once more and it gets stronger.
