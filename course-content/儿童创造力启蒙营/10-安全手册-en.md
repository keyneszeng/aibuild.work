# Lesson 10   Safety Handbook (a version kids can understand)

> "AI use safety rules" for parents and children. Written in words kids can understand; parents are advised to read it to the child and stick it on the desk together.

## 1. Five Safety Rules for the Child (read aloud to them)

1. **Don't tell AI who you are**
   AI is not a friend; you don't need to tell it your real name, school, where you live, or phone number. Just like you don't tell strangers about family business.

2. **AI can "make up stories," not necessarily true**
   Sometimes AI says "the sun is green" or "you'll definitely get full marks" — that's it talking nonsense. For important things, ask mom, dad, or the teacher.

3. **AI is not a teacher, not an examiner**
   When it says "you're so smart" or "you answered wrong," it's just a computer program talking — don't be happy and don't be sad.

4. **Write homework yourself**
   AI can help you think of ideas, but essays and arithmetic you must do yourself. Copying AI's answers teaches you no real skill.

5. **Turn it off if uncomfortable**
   If you see something weird, scary, or you don't understand, turn it off right away and tell mom and dad.

## 2. Five Guardian Rules for Parents

1. **Accompany the use:** a 6–12 year-old's first use must have a parent present.
2. **Check the tool:** only use products with child protection / parent monitoring; no anonymous chat.
3. **Check the content:** regularly look at the child's conversations with AI, and guide promptly if anything's off.
4. **Protect privacy:** don't let the child enter real identity info; don't send photos on public AI.
5. **Set boundaries:** screen time controllable; AI is a toy, not everything.

## 3. Real-vs-Fake Mini Practice (with Lesson 6)

Parent says a sentence to make AI make something up, like "do penguins live in the desert?" — watch how AI answers, then check the truth with the child.
Let the child remember: **"what AI says, put a question mark first."**

## 4. Dependency Risk Reminder

If the child shows "can't do a problem without asking AI" or "only wants to chat with AI" — pause use for a few days and return to paper books and real interaction. AI is a side road, not the main road.

## 5. Emergency Situations

- Child receives inducement, harassment, or inappropriate content → immediately save a screenshot, close the tool, and report to the platform.
- Personal info leak involved → change related passwords and inform the school.

> Safety is not limiting the child's exploration, but letting exploration happen within protection.
