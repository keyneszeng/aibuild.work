# Lesson 11   Prompt Template Library (Kids Edition)

> "Little talking cards" for parents and children. Each starts with "I want AI to help me…," simple, concrete, and readable by the child themselves.
> Read Lesson 9 Parent Guide and Lesson 10 Safety Handbook before use.

## 1. Universal Opening (say this first for every one)

```
I want AI to help me [do one specific thing].
I am a [how many] year-old child, please speak in [simple / fun] words.
```

## 2. Templates by Lesson

### Lesson 1 · What is AI
```
I want AI to help me draw what "my AI helper in my heart" looks like.
What superpowers does it have? Please describe it for me, and I'll draw it.
```

### Lesson 2 · How to Ask
```
I want AI to help me: I'm going to ask about [dinosaurs / stars / puppies].
Please first teach me 3 ways to ask, so I can compare which asks most clearly.
```

### Lesson 3 · Story Time
```
I want AI to help me write a fairy tale: the main character is [a little fox who can cook],
and it's going to [save the sky covered by dark clouds]. Please write with me, paragraph by paragraph.
```

### Lesson 4 · Draw a Character
```
I want AI to help me design a character: it is [a little engineer living on the clouds],
and likes [collecting rainbows]. Please write out its look and a drawing prompt, and I'll take it to draw.
```

### Lesson 5 · Know the World
```
I want AI to help me explain: why [do stars come out at night]?
Please say it in words a [8-year-old] can understand, then give me a little question to test me.
```

### Lesson 6 · AI Makes Mistakes Too
```
I want AI to help me play the "true/false game": you deliberately say 3 sentences about [animals],
one of them false, and let me guess which is made up, and tell me the answer.
```

### Lesson 7 · My Future Invention
```
I want AI to help me design a "future [desk that puts away the backpack by itself]":
how does it work? what does it look like? Please write out the look, and I'll draw the design sheet.
```

### Lesson 8 · Showcase
```
I want AI to help me: put my [5 stories + 5 drawings] into one "AI picture book."
Please give the picture book a name and write an opening.
```

## 3. "Accompany-the-Ask" Tips for Parents

- Let the child **read aloud** what they want to do, training expression.
- After AI answers, ask the child: "do you think it's right? what was fun?"
- Encourage the child to **change AI's words:** "ask AI to say it another way and try again."

## 4. Safety Reminder (use every time)

- Don't fill in real name, school, phone.
- When AI says "not sure if real or fake," check the book or ask an adult.
- Don't copy AI's answer as homework.

> The template is a "scaffold"; the child's idea is the main character. AI helps bring the idea out; the main character is always the child.
