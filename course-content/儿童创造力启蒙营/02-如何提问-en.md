# 02 · How to Ask → Three-Kind Question Comparison Card

## Learning Goals

1. Understand a super important truth: **the clearer you speak, the more accurately AI helps.** Vague words only bring back muddled answers.
2. Recognize "three kinds of questions": unclear ones, clear ones, and clear ones with extra imagination.
3. Make a "three-kind question comparison card" by placing a bad question and a good question side by side.
4. Learn a magic opening sentence: **"I want AI to help me…,"** and say what you want in your heart.

---

## Warm-up Question

> Xiao Ming says to AI: "Help me write something."
> AI is confused: write what? For whom? Long or short? It has to write something random, and Xiao Ming shakes his head after reading.
>
> Xiao Hong says to AI: "I want AI to help me write a little story. The main character is a duckling afraid of water, and in the end it bravely goes into the river — about 5 sentences."
> AI understands right away and immediately writes the story Xiao Hong wanted.
>
> Both said "let AI help," so why such different results? — **Because Xiao Hong said it clearly!**

---

## Key Concept: Three Kinds of Questions

| Type | Looks like | AI's reaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔴 Unclear | "Help me with this," "write something" | guesses around, answers the wrong thing |
| 🟢 Clear | "I want AI to help me draw a red little dragon, in the snow" | does what you said, basically satisfied |
| 🔵 Clear + imagination | "I want AI to help me draw a little dragon wearing a scarf, building a snowman in the snow, in a fairy-tale picture-book style" | accurate and surprising, just like in your head |

> Little secret: clear = say "what + what kind + what you want to happen." Imagination = add a little of your own idea.

---

## Activity: Make a "Three-Kind Question Comparison Card"

Prep: fold a sheet of paper into three columns, or draw three little boxes.

1. **Column 1 (red / unclear):** write a muddled sentence you might have said before, like "help me draw an animal."
2. **Column 2 (green / clear):** rewrite the sentence above clearly, like "I want AI to help me draw an orange little cat, lying on the windowsill in the sun."
3. **Column 3 (blue / add imagination):** add a bit of your own creativity, like "…outside the window little snowflakes are drifting, the whole picture like a warm postcard."
4. **Compare:** look at the three columns and tell your parent "which sentence does AI understand least? which is most like what's in my heart?"
5. **(Little Experts group):** actually ask AI to draw using the column-3 sentence, and see if it's what you wanted.

---

## Parent Tip

- Let the child **say it out loud first, then write it down** — don't rush to typing. "Pouring out" the idea in their head is the first step of question training.
- Use the three questions "what / what kind / what you want to happen" to guide the child to fill in the missing information.
- Praise specific behavior: "you added the idea 'wearing a scarf' and AI drew in your style!" instead of a vague "awesome."

---

## Safety Reminder

- When asking, **only say information related to the task** — don't slip in your name or school.
- If AI didn't draw it right, tell the child: "it's not that you're wrong, the words can still be said more clearly." Turn "failure" into a "try again" game.
- Remind: what AI gives isn't necessarily right; pretty ≠ correct. From next lesson on, we'll keep the habit of "thinking about whether it's right."

## Your Creation

- 📋 **A three-kind question comparison card:** red / green / blue columns, one sentence each, clearly marked "unclear / clear / add imagination."
- 🗣️ **One spoken practice:** the child can blurt out "I want AI to help me… (clear + add imagination)."
- 🌟 **Optional:** stick this card in the study corner; glance at it before each AI use to remind yourself "say it clearly first."

---

## Wrapping Up

A good question = say it clearly (what + what kind + what you want to happen) + add a bit of your imagination. From today on, every time you ask AI for help, start with that magic opening: **"I want AI to help me…."** Say it clearly, and AI will feel like it heard the idea in your heart. Next time, we'll use this skill to make up a fairy tale that belongs only to you!
