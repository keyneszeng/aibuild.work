# Lesson 11 · Master Human-Review Checklist (leave a "human-gate" hook at every step)

> Convergence of every scenario's red lines into one master table. The last step of every task must be "human verification" — AI only drafts.
> This is the camp's most important file — it decides whether you use AI to "gain efficiency" or "plant a landmine."

---

## I. Universal Red Lines (apply to all scenarios)

- [ ] I did not paste company secrets, customer privacy, or unpublished data into an unsafe tool
- [ ] For any price / policy / commitment content, what AI wrote is marked [to be confirmed by me] and I fill the real value
- [ ] All key numbers (amounts / ratios / rankings) I verified against the original table or calculator
- [ ] Before publishing externally, I read it once and edited it once — not forwarding AI's original text directly
- [ ] For high-risk decisions (medical / legal / financial / HR), AI only organizes info; the conclusion is set by a professional

---

## II. Scenario-Specific Checklists

### Writing / Weekly report / Plan
- [ ] Facts and data came from me; AI did not fabricate
- [ ] Tone fits company / brand
- [ ] No typos or ambiguity

### Meeting / Minutes / Follow-up
- [ ] To-dos and owners are truthful
- [ ] Deadlines reasonable
- [ ] Internal info distributed internally only

### Sales / Support
- [ ] Cases / data in scripts are real (no fabricated winning stories)
- [ ] Price, discount, delivery, warranty policy given by me only; AI makes no commitments
- [ ] Support replies strictly based on our materials; where none, honestly say "I'll confirm and reply"
- [ ] Customer names / phones / chat logs masked

### Content / Operations
- [ ] No exaggerated efficacy, no clickbait, no fake user reviews
- [ ] Compliant with Advertising Law and platform rules
- [ ] Data claims verifiable

### Spreadsheets / Data
- [ ] Key numbers verified
- [ ] Anomaly judgments based on real rows, not AI inference
- [ ] Sensitive details not leaked

### Management / HR / Hiring
- [ ] JD has no age / gender etc. discriminatory language
- [ ] Salary and benefits real; no AI-fabricated "fake benefits"
- [ ] Interview questions layered reasonably, can differentiate candidates
- [ ] Retrospective root cause and owner confirmed by me
- [ ] Candidate / employee info strictly confidential

---

## III. "Human-Gate" Placeholder Convention

Use these placeholders consistently in prompts and outputs to remind yourself where human input is mandatory:

| Placeholder | Meaning | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| `[to be confirmed by me]` | Facts AI cannot decide | price, policy, commitment |
| `[needs my check]` | Numbers / inferences AI computed | amount, ratio, trend |
| `[to be filled by me]` | Items I must assign | owner, root cause, decision |
| `[masked]` | Privacy already hidden | name, phone, client name |

---

## IV. Usage Discipline (suggested to keep on your desk)

1. **Think before asking**: be clear what you want before letting AI act.
2. **Share materials, not secrets**: when giving AI materials, delete sensitive fields first.
3. **Do the arithmetic yourself**: verify any number AI gives.
4. **Read once before publishing**: an AI draft is not a finished product.
5. **Keep a trace**: store important outputs locally; don't leave them only in the chat box.

> Remember one line: **human judges, AI assists. The steering wheel and the brakes are always in your hands.**
