# Lesson 7 · Management & Hiring → Job Descriptions / Interview Questions / Team Retrospectives

## I. Learning Goals

- **Knowledge**: Understand that the document work of "hiring" and "leading" can be massively compressed by AI; master the "give key points → AI expands" JD-writing method; use AI to generate structured interview questions from a role profile; turn a retrospective meeting into a "problem — cause — action" list.
- **Skill**: Produce a professional JD in 30 minutes; generate 8–10 layered interview questions for a role; organize retrospective audio/notes into a trackable improvement table.
- **Output**: A *Hiring Package* (JD + interview questions + scoring dimensions) + a *Team-Retrospective Improvement Table*.

> This lesson references LLM-Universe's "generate structured documents with templated prompts" and Prompt-Engineering-Guide's "role-play + explicit output structure" techniques.

## II. Warm-up Question

> Hiring someone, but the JD takes half a day and still reads like a copy-paste?
> Interview questions loop the same few lines, and afterward you can't compare candidates?
> Two-hour retrospective, conclusion "pay attention next time" — and then there is no next time?

Half of management is "writing the standard clearly." AI helps turn the requirements in your head into reusable documents.

## III. Core Concepts

### 1. JD = expand key points, not invent from nothing
You give AI "role title + 3 core duties + what kind of person we want," and it expands into a professional JD. You own "where the requirements come from"; AI handles "making it look proper."

### 2. Layered interview questions: behavioral / professional / culture
Have AI generate questions in three categories — behavioral (how they did in the past) / professional (can they do it) / culture (good fit) — and you pick. This is the basic skill of structured interviewing.

### 3. Retrospective = problem — cause — action
Give the retrospective content to AI and have it sort into three columns. Key point: what AI gives is a "draft"; the "root cause" and "who's responsible" must be decided by you.

### 4. Red line: no discriminatory or untruthful wording
No age/gender discriminatory terms in the JD; salary range and duties must be real — don't publish AI-fabricated "fake benefits."

## IV. Real Work Scenario Demo

### Case 1: A JD in half a day
**Background**: Hiring a "community operations" specialist.
**AI output**: A professional JD with [5 job duties / 4 requirements / plus-points / what we offer].
**Human gate**: You fill real info for salary and reporting line; delete any untruthful words AI may add like "ultimate flexibility" or "generous options."

### Case 2: Retrospective becomes a list
**Background**: Notes from a project-delay retrospective.
**AI output**: | Problem | Root cause (to confirm) | Improvement action | Owner (to fill) |.
**Human gate**: You decide the root cause, you assign the owner — AI doesn't make HR decisions for you.

## V. Hands-on Task

- **Background**: You are hiring or just finished a retrospective.
- **Input**: Role key points, or a retrospective record.
- **Steps**:
  1. Use the "JD Template" to generate a hiring package.
  2. Use the "Interview Questions Template" to generate layered questions.
  3. Use the "Retrospective Template" to organize the improvement table.
- **Expected output**: *Hiring Package* + *Team-Retrospective Improvement Table*.

## VI. Prompt Template

```
You are now playing the role of: a senior HR consultant
I am hiring: [role title]
Core duties (I'll dictate): [3–5 points]
The kind of person we want: [traits]
Please output: a JD with four sections [Job Duties / Requirements / Plus-points / What We Offer];
then 10 interview questions, tagged by [behavioral / professional / culture].
Constraints: no discriminatory language of any kind; salary and benefits only what I provide, no fabrication.
```

## VII. Human Review Checklist

- [ ] JD has no discriminatory language, salary is real
- [ ] Interview questions cover all three types and can differentiate candidates
- [ ] Retrospective root cause and owner confirmed by me
- [ ] No candidate privacy leaked externally

## VIII. Common Mistakes

1. Publishing a JD with AI-fabricated "fake benefits."
2. Interview questions all useless ones like "your biggest weakness."
3. Letting AI guess the retrospective root cause, never landing it.
4. Using AI to generate biased screening criteria.
