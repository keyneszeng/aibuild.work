# Lesson 9 · Toolkit for Six Roles (Admin / Sales / Support / Operations / HR / Sole Proprietor)

> 10+ real work scenarios per role. Each scenario includes [raw task / AI collaboration / sample Prompt / output sample / human review checklist / privacy-risk reminder].
> Core principle: **human judges, AI assists; no code, API, or model parameters involved.**

---

## I. Admin & Clerical (10 scenarios)

**Scenario 1: Write a meeting notice**
- Raw task: Send a notice for the Wednesday regular meeting.
- AI collaboration: Give key points → expand into a formal notice.
- Sample Prompt: "You are admin; write a meeting notice: time Wednesday 15:00, location 3rd floor, topic Q3 retrospective, bring weekly report. Formal tone."
- Output sample: A complete notice with title, time/place, agenda, and signature.
- Human check: Time/place accurate, attendees correct.
- Privacy: No classified agenda details sent out.

**Scenario 2: Organize meeting minutes**
- Raw task: Turn a 1-hour recording into minutes.
- AI collaboration: Paste transcript → extract "decision / to-do / owner."
- Sample Prompt: "Organize the following meeting notes into a four-column table: [decision / to-do / owner / deadline]."
- Human check: To-dos and owners are truthful.
- Privacy: Minutes contain internal info — distribute internally only.

**Scenario 3: Monthly duty roster**
- Raw task: Schedule 5 people on rotation.
- AI collaboration: Give rules → produce table.
- Sample Prompt: "Build the August duty roster, 5 people rotating, avoid two on weekend leave at once."
- Human check: No conflicts, compliant.

**Scenario 4: Draft policy explanation**
- Raw task: Rewrite a new attendance policy into employee-friendly language.
- AI collaboration: Give clauses → plain-language version.
- Human check: Doesn't distort the original policy.

**Scenario 5: PPT script**
- Raw task: Turn reporting points into per-slide copy.
- AI collaboration: Give outline → paginated copy.
- Human check: Data is real.

**Scenario 6: Email sorting & reply drafts**
- Raw task: Classify 20 emails then reply.
- AI collaboration: Paste emails → tag "urgent / routine / spam" + draft replies.
- Human check: Urgency judged correctly, outgoing tone right.

**Scenario 7: Event flow checklist**
- Raw task: Promote a department team-building.
- AI collaboration: Give headcount → flow + material list.
- Human check: Budget and venue real.

**Scenario 8: Official-document format proofread**
- Raw task: Check a request memo's format.
- AI collaboration: Paste text → list format issues.
- Human check: Final review against unit standards.

**Scenario 9: Weekly-report consolidation**
- Raw task: Merge department weekly reports into one.
- AI collaboration: Paste several → extract key points.
- Human check: No key items missed.

**Scenario 10: Common FAQ**
- Raw task: Organize admin questions employees often ask.
- AI collaboration: Give Q&A → build a library.
- Human check: Answers accurate and compliant.
