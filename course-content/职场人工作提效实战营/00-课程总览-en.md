# AI Productivity Bootcamp — Course Overview

> A hands-on AI course for everyday professionals. No coding, no jargon-stacking — it solves one thing only: **getting you to actually use AI in your daily work, starting today**.

---

## I. Course Positioning

This bootcamp is the "getting-work-done" track of the AI Skills Upgrade curriculum, aimed at ordinary working professionals. It serves: administrative staff, sales, customer service, operations, HR, finance, sole proprietors, and small-team managers.

We assume you have **no programming skills whatsoever**. You don't need to understand model parameters, APIs, or training principles. If you can use WeChat, operate a computer, and state a request clearly, you can learn this.

The course's core principle is one sentence:

> **Human judgment, AI assistance. AI is your co-pilot for saving time — the steering wheel and the brakes are always in your hands.**

## II. Target Users & Typical Pain Points

| Role | What drains them most each day | Where AI helps immediately |
|---|---|---|
| Administrative | Drafting notices, scheduling, organizing meeting notes | Drafting + formatting + summaries |
| Sales | Finding pitches, following up clients, writing proposals | Pitch generation + objection handling + follow-up reminders |
| Customer service | Repetitive Q&A, handling complaints | Standard replies + emotional soothing + case accumulation |
| Operations | Topic selection, copywriting, script editing | Topic library + multiple copy variants + script frameworks |
| HR | Writing JDs, screening resumes, designing interview questions | JD templates + interview questions + review frameworks |
| Finance | Reconciling accounts, building spreadsheets, writing explanations | Data summaries + anomaly detection + business phrasing |
| Sole proprietor | Doing the work of an entire company alone | All-scenario assistant |
| Small-team manager | Reviews, communication, decisions | Meeting notes + reviews + decision lists |

**Eight common pain points** (the whole course is built around solving them):

1. Writing the same things over and over is exhausting.
2. Not knowing what to write in weekly or monthly reports.
3. Two-hour meetings you can't remember and can't act on.
4. Fumbling when clients say no; pitches depend on inspiration.
5. Needing content but unable to come up with a headline in half a day.
6. Piles of spreadsheets that reveal no insight.
7. Hiring with no standard; reviews based on gut feeling.
8. Learning lots of AI but still unable to use it back on the job.

## III. Learning Goals (What You'll Take Away)

- **Knowledge**: Understand what AI can do, what it can't, and when it will "make things up" (hallucinate).
- **Skill**: Master a **standard Prompt structure** so you can write usable instructions for any work task on your own.
- **Output**: Eight lessons produce eight ready-to-use job materials (scenario lists, weekly report templates, meeting minutes, sales scripts, topic libraries, data-dashboard phrasing, recruitment kits, a personal AI work-assistant spec) + six role toolkits.

## V. Red Lines (Must Remember)

1. **AI makes mistakes — don't trust it blindly.** It sometimes fabricates data, names, regulations, and citations. Anything involving money, contracts, time, law, or medicine must be verified by a human.
2. **Don't share secrets.** Never upload company secrets, customer privacy, unpublished data, or ID numbers / phone numbers / contract text to any AI tool. Use "masked" information instead (e.g., "a certain client," "December data").
3. **Human judgment, AI assistance.** AI gives you drafts and suggestions; the final decisions, external messaging, and accountability are yours.
4. **Protect business information.** Competitor strategy, pricing, client lists, and internal structures are sensitive — don't feed them to public AI.
5. **Keep things traceable.** For important deliverables, keep both the "AI draft + your edits" versions to support review and accountability.

## VI. Standard Prompt Structure (Used in Every Lesson)

> This is the unified "fill-in-the-blank" template for the whole course. If you don't know how to write a Prompt, just fill this in.

```
You are now playing the role of: [role]
My background is: [background]
The task I need to complete is: [task]
The target user / reader is: [reader]
Existing materials are as follows: [materials]
Please output: [output]
Output format: [format]
Constraints: [constraints]
First ask me 3 key questions; if the information is sufficient, start directly.
(Workplace edition add-on: please output a version directly usable in work scenarios, with a checklist attached)
```

**How to use**: Replace the bracketed parts with your real situation and copy the whole block to AI. Lesson 1 will walk you through it until it's second nature.
