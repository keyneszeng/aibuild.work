# Lesson 05 · AI-Assisted Résumé & Interview

## 1. Learning Goals

- **Knowledge**: Understand that AI in job-seeking is a "polishing assistant", not a "ghostwriting gunman"; master the methods of "telling experience with STAR structure" and "reverse-deriving résumé focus from the job JD"; know that AI-fabricated internships/projects must not be written into the résumé (integrity red line).
- **Capability**: Rewrite a vague experience into a résumé entry with data and action; optimize résumé focus for a specific job JD; generate mock-interview Q&A and self-assess.
- **Artifact**: An "Optimized Résumé (key-points highlighted)" + a "Mock Interview Record (Q/A/improvement)".

> This lesson references Generative-AI-For-Beginners' "using AI for text rewriting and structuring", Prompt-Engineering-Guide's "role-play + iterative optimization" techniques, reconstructed with real job-seeking scenarios.

## 2. Warm-up Question

> Résumé says "responsible for project management", why isn't the interviewer interested?
> The same you, but one résumé for every job?
> Asked in interview "tell me your most challenging thing", freeze on the spot?

Résumé and interview test not "how impressive you are" but "whether you can explain your impressiveness clearly". AI helps translate your experience into the language headhunters and interviewers love.

## 3. Core Concepts

### 1. STAR: give experience a skeleton
Situation / Task / Action / Result. AI helps turn a running account into STAR, but **numbers in results must be real**.

### 2. JD reverse-derivation: cater to preferences without faking
Give the target job JD to AI, let it mark "the 3 capabilities this job values most", then you move the corresponding experience forward and write it thoroughly.

### 3. Mock interview: use AI as the interviewer
Let AI play a strict interviewer, probing based on your résumé. You practice expression, it gives feedback. This is the real usage of "iterative optimization".

### 4. Red line: don't fabricate experience
Internships you didn't do, projects you didn't join — AI can fabricate them but you cannot write them. Once integrity breaks, the cost far exceeds one Offer.

## 4. Real-scenario Case Demonstration

### Case 1: Experience rewriting
**Original**: "Participated in campus official-account operation."
**AI rewrite**: "Operated a university-level official account (8000+ followers), independently pitched 12 articles, single-article max reads 12k, brought 1500+ new-student follows."
**Human check**: Do you really have those 8000/12k/1500 numbers? If not, don't write them.

### Case 2: Mock interview
**AI asks**: "You just said responsible for data analysis, what method, what conclusion?"
**After you answer** AI comments: "Conclusion clear but missing 'so what' — next time add one line of business impact."
**Value**: exposes expression weaknesses in advance.

## 5. Hands-on Task

- **Background**: You have a résumé draft + a target job JD.
- **Input**: current résumé text, target JD.
- **Steps**:
  1. Use the "résumé optimization template" to rewrite 3 experiences into STAR.
  2. Use the "JD reverse-derivation template" to reorder résumé focus.
  3. Use the "mock interview template" to run 5 rounds of Q&A and record improvements.
- **Expected output**: "Optimized Résumé" + "Mock Interview Record".

## 6. Prompt Template

```
You are now playing: senior career coach
My background: [major/year/experience draft]
Target job JD: [paste]
Please:
1. Rewrite my 3 experiences into STAR structure, use [real data] placeholders for result numbers for me to fill;
2. Mark the 3 capabilities this JD values most, suggest which experiences I should move forward;
3. Based on my résumé, simulate 5 questions the interviewer might probe, with scoring dimensions.
Constraint: never fabricate experiences I haven't done.
```

## 7. Human Review Checklist

- [ ] All résumé numbers are real and answerable
- [ ] No fabricated internships/projects/skills
- [ ] JD focus reflected and not faked
- [ ] I have digested the mock interview improvement points

## 8. Common Mistakes

1. Letting AI fabricate "impressive-looking" fake experiences.
2. One résumé for all jobs without adjustment.
3. Only changing wording not structure, still like a running account.
4. Practiced mock interview but didn't review.
