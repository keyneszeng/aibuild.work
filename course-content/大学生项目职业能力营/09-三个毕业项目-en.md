# Lesson 09 · Three Capstone Projects → Detailed Designs You Can Start Directly

**Suitable for**: university students, graduate students, new graduates who want to graduate with one complete project and put it in their portfolio
**Suggested duration**: 120 minutes (choose one project to deepen, or read all three to choose one)

## 1. Course Goals
After this lesson, you will be able to:
1. **Knowledge**: Master three instances of the complete project-design paradigm "start from pain point, end with verifiable Demo";
2. **Capability**: Select one of three candidate capstones, apply Lesson 06's plan framework to complete your own proposal;
3. **Artifact**: The **detailed design** of the selected project (background, problem definition, solution, process steps, expected outcomes), ready to enter Lesson 07 for prototyping.

---

## 2. Warm-up Question
> You must submit a project at camp end but don't know what to do. This lesson gives you three directions "already validated for value and completable by students": résumé-interview assistant, study-material organizer, industry-research assistant. Pick the one closest to you, start today.

---

## 3. Core Concepts
All three projects follow the same paradigm (see Lessons 06/10): **real pain point → quantifiable value → no-code executable → demonstrable acceptance**. They cover three high-frequency scenarios "job-seeking" / "learning" / "research", with increasing difficulty, but all completable in 2–4 weeks with no-code/low-code. On technology selection, all three suit RAG (Q&A based on exclusive materials) or rule + Prompt combinations, prioritizing Coze/Dify/Feishu Multi-dimensional Tables, and Streamlit when file-upload analysis is needed.

---

## 4. Case Demonstration (real scenarios)
- **Direction 1 validated**: a student built a "résumé checkup + mock interview" agent with Coze, showed it as a portfolio piece when applying for internships; the interviewer experienced it on the spot, direct plus.
- **Direction 2 validated**: a grad student used Feishu Multi-dimensional Tables to break 200 pages of course PDF into "knowledge points + self-test questions", doubling final-review efficiency.
- **Direction 3 validated**: a business-major student used AI to compress a week of industry research into one day, producing a report with source links, adopted by the internship mentor.

## 6. Prompt Template (project selection version)
```
My situation: [major/year/materials at hand], time about [X weeks].
Based on the three directions "AI résumé-interview assistant / AI study-material organizer / AI industry-research assistant",
help me judge which suits me best, with reasons, and list 3 materials I need to prepare in advance for each option.
```

- [ ] I marked the technical path and reasons

---

## 8. Common Mistakes
1. **Want all three**: greed leads to all half-done, better do one thoroughly.
2. **Choose flashiest not closest**: chose the direction with the least materials, stuck finding data later.
3. **Ignore acceptance**: no standard for "what counts as success".
4. **Underestimate privacy**: upload résumés with personal info / unpublished materials directly to third-party platforms without handling.

## Appendix: Detailed Designs of the Three Capstone Projects

### Project 1 · AI Résumé-Interview Assistant
- **Background**: When job-seeking, students have weak résumé expression, don't understand job matching, panic in live interviews. General tools don't fit personal real experience.
- **Problem definition**: Help users rewrite a real experience into a STAR résumé entry within 30 minutes, and complete one structured mock interview for the target JD.
- **Solution**: Knowledge base = user's real experience + target job JD; build agent with Coze/Dify, with three skills "résumé optimization" / "mock interview" / "gap analysis"; AI can only optimize expression, must not fabricate experience (set red-line validation).
- **Process steps**: ① collect own experience and JD; ② build knowledge base and Prompt; ③ test per skill; ④ record 8–10 interview rounds; ⑤ output before/after comparison + interview record.
- **Expected outcome**: "Résumé Optimization Comparison Table" + "Mock Interview Record", shareable Demo link, as the portfolio's core card.

### Project 2 · AI Study-Material Organizer
- **Background**: Course/paper materials are many and scattered, slow reading, unfocused review.
- **Problem definition**: Automatically organize PPT/PDF/notes into a structured review pack of "knowledge points + self-test questions + source page numbers".
- **Solution**: Use Feishu Multi-dimensional Tables' "AI fields" to generate summaries and questions per material segment; or use Dify to upload materials for RAG Q&A; keep source annotations to control hallucination.
- **Process steps**: ① organize materials into base; ② configure extraction/question-generation Prompt; ③ batch-produce and manually spot-check accuracy; ④ make a shareable review table / Q&A assistant.
- **Expected outcome**: a structured review pack + Demo, reflecting "connect AI back to professional learning".

### Project 3 · AI Industry-Research Assistant
- **Background**: Internships/coursework often need to quickly understand an industry; manual retrieval is time-consuming and easily misses sources.
- **Problem definition**: Input an industry name, produce a structured report within 10 minutes containing "top companies, key trends, risks, each conclusion with source link".
- **Solution**: RAG + rule workflow (Coze/Dify or Streamlit), first do retrieval aggregation then let the model produce by template, forcing each conclusion to carry a citation.
- **Process steps**: ① define report template and source whitelist; ② build retrieval+generation flow; ③ test 3 industries, verify source authenticity; ④ export report templated.
- **Expected outcome**: a reusable industry-research workflow + 3 sample reports, strongly proving "research ability + AI execution ability".
